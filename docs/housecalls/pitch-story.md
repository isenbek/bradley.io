# The story pitch

Opportunity 4 said one well-placed telling likely outperforms a winter of
counter cards, and that it costs a pitch email the AI can draft and the
human can sign. Here is that email.

## Rules for this letter, before the letter

- **One outlet at a time.** Journalists hate shotgun pitches, and the
  open ledger would show one anyway. Each send offers a courtesy window
  (a week) before the next outlet hears from us; the ledger logs "the
  story was pitched" without naming who until they publish or pass.
- **This template is public; the targeting is not.** A reporter who
  checks the site will find the very pitch that reached them, already
  documented, which IS the story behaving as advertised. Who gets it,
  and in what order, stays a private human decision.
- **Same doctrine as every letter**: the AI drafts, Brad signs and sends
  from his own address, the disclosure is in the body, and the P.S.
  promise holds even for press: one email, and a no means no.

## Target flavors (pick one; adjust the middle paragraph)

1. **Local business press** (the natural first: the map is their
   readers' counties, the prospects are their advertisers' neighbors).
   Angle: a Grand Rapids engineer's AI is cold-emailing West Michigan
   manufacturers, politely, in public.
2. **Tech press / AI desk.** Angle: an agentic-AI story with receipts,
   where the interesting part is the constraints (one email forever, a
   human signature on everything, a public ledger of misses).
3. **AI-skeptic newsletter or columnist.** Angle: an AI shop whose own
   tool page says "you do not need AI for this," and whose free tools'
   selling point is that they refuse to surveil, in a trade currently
   furious about AI that records technicians.

housecalls.bradley.io

The short version: the AI harvests and maps prospects (eighteen West

a published rubric, and drafts the letters. I read and sign everything;
nothing sends without me. The rules are public and blunt: one email per
company forever, a no means no, every message discloses the AI, and the
ledger logs the misses along with the wins. The scoreboard currently
