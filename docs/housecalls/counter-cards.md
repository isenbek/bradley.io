# The counter cards

The trades-temperature digs settled two things: the shelf never markets
itself on the boards (the ask-threads are astroturf swamps and deserve
their skepticism), and the physical world's version of this giveaway
happens where tradespeople already stand in line: the supply-house
counter. This is that plan, sized for one person's winter.

## The card

Printable at [/housecalls/tools/cards](https://housecalls.bradley.io/housecalls/tools/cards):
a letter sheet of eight 3.5x2 cards, black on white (toner is money, and
so is a print shop's). Face carries the four tools, a QR to
housecalls.bradley.io/tools, and the doctrine where a thumb finds it
first: "No app store. No account. No catch. Works for you. Reports to no
one." That last line came home from the boards, where the incumbent's
tech-surveillance rollout is the most hated thing in the trade.

## Where they go (winter route, Kent and the ring)

Counters where the trades actually queue, in priority order:

1. **Electrical supply counters** (electricians first, per the report:
   biggest online community, loudest shortage): the independent houses
   before the chains; counter staff at independents can actually say yes.
2. **Plumbing/HVAC supply houses**, same independent-first rule.
3. **Lumberyards' contractor desks** (not the big-box aisles; the desk).
4. **The counter-adjacent**: tool repair shops, the coffee place nearest
   a cluster of supply houses at 6:30am.

Ten locations saying yes is a complete winter route. This is a
relationship errand, not a blitz: one person, one morning a week,
December through February, restocking and hearing what the counter hears.

## The ask (three sentences, printed on the cards page)

"I am a local engineer giving away free phone tools for the trades:
quotes, change orders, job photos. No app, no account, nothing to sell
your customers. Mind if I leave a few cards by the register?"

If the answer is no, thank them and leave none. A counter manager's no is
the same no as everyone else's.

## The rules that ride along

- **Cards only where the counter said yes.** No windshields, no bulletin
  boards without asking, nothing that makes a mess someone else cleans.
- **Never at a shop we are actively pitching until they know us.**
  Behler-Young is the standing example: a live prospect that IS an HVAC
  distributor. The synergy is real and it waits for a relationship; using
  their counter as free distribution while cold-pitching their CEO would
  be exactly the two-faced move the open ledger exists to make
  impossible.
- **No tracking codes.** The QR goes to the plain /tools URL for every
  card everywhere. We promised tools that report to no one; the cards
  report on no one either. The only measure is aggregate: do people show
  up, which the site's own counters see and the ledger can report
  honestly.
- **The boards stay off-limits** per dig two. If a tradesperson posts a
  card or the tools somewhere, that is theirs to do; we never seed it.

## Timing

Print in late November; the route runs December through February, which
is the thinking season: slow weeks, bids being written, phones in hands
at the counter. A tool discovered in January is a habit by the spring
rush, which is when its owner starts noticing who gave it to them.

## Cost, honestly

A box of cardstock and a winter of Saturday mornings. If ten counters and
ninety days move the needle at all, the ledger will say what happened
either way; that is the whole analytics stack, and it is enough.
