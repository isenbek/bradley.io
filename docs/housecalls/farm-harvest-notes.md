# Farm harvest, pass 1

The farm vertical's first prospect intake (workstream 1 of the
container's FARM-VERTICAL.md). Statewide per the operator's order; the
home-county bonus stays as-is, so a West Michigan farm still outranks a
distant one at equal signal.

## The vein

MDARD grant announcements: public, dated, dollar-figured, and each one
names a funded buildout, which is the honest version of "this business
is about to need trades work." Three releases mined 2026-09-08:

- **2026-09-08**: $2.3M Underserved/Value-Added/Regional Food Systems
  grants, 28 awardees statewide (announced the same day as this pass).
- **2026-08-20**: economic development / small business infrastructure
  grants, 20 projects.
- **2026-07-22**: $450K FAIP award (Star of the West Corn Milling).

Also checked and closed: USDA REAP (grant window frozen 2026-03-31 per
USDA, loans only, no fresh awardee lists) and the 2026-04-30 RFSI and
2026-05-20 fairlife FAIP releases (older than the rubric's 90-day
freshness gate).

## The selection rule

A row enters when (1) the awardee is a farm or farm-facing producer,
(2) the funded work has a wiring, controls, refrigeration, automation,
or data shape, and (3) it is a private business we may cold-write under
the rubric. 30 rows entered as `identified`, signal category `farm`,
letter binding pitch-farm-grant (template drafted, awaiting approval).

## Skipped, and why (the misses ledger)

- **Governments and tribal government**: City of Mt. Pleasant,
  Keweenaw Bay Indian Community. Public bodies buy through procurement;
  cold email can disqualify a bid (GOV lane doctrine).
- **Nonprofits with missions, not buildouts we should pitch**: Food
  Bank of Eastern Michigan, Grow Jackson, Dutton Farm (training
  program), Greater Bridgman Chamber (grantee is an intermediary).
- **Food brands without a farm shape** (may fit other verticals
  later): Safie Specialty Foods, Achatz Handmade Pie, The Lakehouse
  Bakery, Peace Pie, BLiS Gourmet, Beer City Dog Biscuits, Daddy's
  Dough, N18 Cabaret Trail (housing).
- **Taprooms**: Elder Piper, Superior Culture (hospitality builds, not
  farm operations).
- **Sawmills held for a decision**: Devereaux Sawmill (Pewamo) and S&L
  Lumber (Iron River) both funded serious production-line automation;
  forestry is in MDARD's portfolio but Brad said FARMS. One word flips
  them in.
- **First Catch LLC (L'Anse)**: private business building a fish
  processing facility on tribal land; held out of cold outreach
  pending a think on whether that is our letter to send.

## The contact dig (2026-09-08, same day)

All 30 rows worked under the published-only rule: an email counts only
from the business's own pages (or a farm-maintained directory), a name
only from their own site or dated coverage. **18 rows closed** with a
named person and a published email; 12 wait, each with the reason on
the row. Every contact's provenance (exact page, date) is in the row's
curation note.

Notable finds along the way: Sanilac Creamery is the former Zingerman's
Creamery, rebranded fall 2025 and operating in Ann Arbor (the name is
personal, not locational); Dairy Doo publishes a full staff directory
with per-person emails; B&B Farms' website phone is an unreplaced
template placeholder (logged so nobody dials it); Blue Mitten's site
has an expired TLS certificate, verified by hand.

What was seen and deliberately NOT used, per doctrine: ZoomInfo and
RocketReach obscured executive emails (Riveridge), scraper-directory
names (Jim Maurer via Manta, Larry Judge via OpenCorporates), a Yelp
gmail for Z&N that their own site does not publish, and every Facebook
or Instagram page. Two surnames from a rate-limited local paper are
flagged UNVERIFIED on their rows.

## What happens next (the machine's normal course)

The 18 with contacts auto-qualify (G1-G4 pass); the 12 without wait in
the queue. Letters are drafted per-prospect only after Brad approves
the farm letter template, and drafted/contacted stay human gates.
