What You're Seeing06

The knife edge. Below p*, gravity loses: the pulse implodes through the origin and re-explodes to infinity, leaving flat space. Above p*, gravity wins: 2m/r pins to 1 and a horizon forms. Exactly at p* sits the critical solution, a universal attractor that has forgotten everything about your initial data.

The echoes. Watch φ(0,t) on a near-critical run: the field rings at the origin, each oscillation ~31× faster and smaller than the last. Why it matters for the blank zone. The critical solution is a naked, self-similar spacetime region where curvature climbs without bound: classically it cascades through every scale on the ruler, from the star all the way down toward Planck. It's the cleanest mathematical thread we have that runs continuously into the unmapped basement. Whatever cuts the cascade off is, by definition, quantum gravity.
