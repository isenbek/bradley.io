01 / IDENTITY

The wordmark

A rounded, friendly lowercase logotype with a fluid connection between the bowls. The dot of the i is set a shade lighter to give the mark a touch of organic life. Blue is the primary lockup.

Do

  • Keep clear space of at least the height of the o on all sides.
  • Use Bio Blue on light/cream backgrounds; reverse to white on dark or blue fills.
  • Minimum width 96px digital · 24mm print to keep the dot legible.
  • Pair the mark with one accent color at a time, never all four.

Don't

  • Don't recolor the wordmark outside the four approved hues.
  • Don't stretch, skew, outline, or add drop shadows to the mark.
  • Don't place the blue mark on busy photos without a solid backing.
  • Don't typeset "bradley.io" in a different font than the body family.

06 / VOICE

How bradley.io sounds

Curious and hands-on. Plain-spoken about hard things. The voice of a builder who'd rather show you the wiring than hide it.

Clear, not clever

We say: "Here's exactly how it works."
Not: buzzword soup or hype.

Warm & direct

We say: friendly, first-person, confident.
Not: stiff corporate distance.

Technical with a wink

We say: precise specs, dry humor welcome.
Not: dumbed-down or joyless.
